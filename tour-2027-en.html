The journey

One story,
from spring to September

Futura sets off from the Adriatic, runs down the coast of Puglia, reaches Naples for the America’s Cup, then Amalfi, Capri, Sardinia, Liguria and the Côte d’Azur. She stays a few days in each port, meets people, and sets off again with new guests. The journey is the story.
