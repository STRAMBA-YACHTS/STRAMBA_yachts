Why

A new way of being at sea, and of telling it

We make this journey to show that the sea can be lived in another way: freer, more comfortable, more respectful of the planet. And to tell it together with those who share this outlook.

The journey

A summer
to discover

From spring to September Futura heads up the Adriatic, runs down the coast of Puglia, reaches Naples, then Amalfi, Capri, Sardinia and the Côte d’Azur. She stays for days, welcomes visitors aboard, and sets off again with new guests. The journey is the story.
